# Ride Mitra — User Guide (English)

## 1. Student Guide
1. **Login:** Enter your 10-digit phone number and submit the 6-digit OTP.
2. **Booking an Auto:**
   - Select Pickup and Drop stops along the Lavel Stop ↔ GIT & Hostel corridor.
   - The upfront fixed fare is loaded dynamically from the fare table (no typing, no bargaining).
   - Select Passenger count (1–3) and Ride Type (Private Full Auto or Shared Ride).
   - Tap **"Confirm Booking"**.
3. **Live Ride Tracking:**
   - Watch real-time status transitions: *Searching → Driver Assigned → Arriving → On Trip → Completed*.
   - Tap to call the assigned driver directly or check their rating and auto plate.
4. **Emergency Safety (SOS):**
   - Tap the red SOS button during an active ride to alert campus security and system administrators with GPS coordinates.
5. **Rating & Complaints:**
   - Rate the trip from 1 to 5 stars with tag pills (*Polite, Safe Driving, Clean Auto*).
   - Report issues (overcharging, cancellation) via the complaints modal.

---

## 2. Driver Guide
1. **Free Registration:**
   - Fill in your name, phone number, and vehicle registration number (e.g. `MH 08 AB 1234`).
   - The registration is 100% free with 0% commission.
2. **Turn Rotation Queue:**
   - Tap the large **"Go Online"** toggle.
   - See your live queue position: *"You are #1 in line"* based on fair wait-time rotation.
3. **Incoming Ride Alert:**
   - A loud audio chime rings for 30 seconds displaying the Pickup, Drop, Passenger count, and cash fare.
   - Tap **"Accept"** to claim the ride.
4. **Trip Lifecycle:**
   - Tap **"I Have Arrived"** upon reaching pickup.
   - Tap **"Start Trip"** when passenger boards.
   - Tap **"Complete Trip"** upon arrival and collect 100% cash fare directly.
